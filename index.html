What is this?

Alles Hockey

A Swiss ice-hockey board for tonight, not someday: find or post last-minute players, goalies, and ice time — plus sell or buy used gear.

What you can do

  • Last Minute — short-lived “HS Cards”: who is needed, when, where, level. Contact shows only when someone taps through.
  • 2ndChance — used sticks, pads, skates and more; listings expire fast so the board stays fresh.
  • Map — see what’s nearby.

Closed beta, invite only. Same product family as hockeyschlampe.ch (browser). Storefront: alles-hockey.ch.
