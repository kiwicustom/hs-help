Two steps

Email code → Create PIN

Mail codes expire. The PIN is what you use after logout — same pattern as kcal.

Step 1 — Email code

  1. 1

    Enter the invite email.

  2. 2

    Paste the 8-character code from the mail (or tap Email me a code).

  3. 3

    Tap Continue.

Step 2 — Create PIN

  1. 1

    Choose a 4–6 digit PIN and confirm.

  2. 2

    After logout: email + PIN (no new mail needed).

Forgot PIN? Use “Email me a code”, then you can set a new PIN after sign-in.
